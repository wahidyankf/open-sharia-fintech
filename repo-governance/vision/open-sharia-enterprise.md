# Open Sharia Enterprise Vision

This is the foundational, ecosystem-level vision document: why Open Sharia Enterprise exists and what change it seeks. It is the only vision document; products state their own purpose in their specifications.

## Contents

- [Why We Exist](./open-sharia-enterprise/why-we-exist.md) — The founding purpose — democratizing Shariah-compliant enterprise technology. Use when explaining to a newcomer why this project exists at all.
- [The Problem We Solve](./open-sharia-enterprise/the-problem-we-solve.md) — The current state of Islamic enterprise technology and the barriers it creates for developers, businesses, and individuals. Use when explaining what is broken about the status quo that Open Sharia Enterprise addresses.
- [The Change We Seek](./open-sharia-enterprise/the-change-we-seek.md) — The world Open Sharia Enterprise envisions — where anyone can build Shariah-compliant solutions and ethical business is the default. Use when describing the future state Open Sharia Enterprise is working toward.
- [Scope: Beyond Just Code](./open-sharia-enterprise/scope-beyond-just-code.md) — What Open Sharia Enterprise provides as a movement — technical infrastructure, education, and community — and what it deliberately is not. Use when clarifying what Open Sharia Enterprise does and does not claim to be.
- [Islamic Foundation](./open-sharia-enterprise/islamic-foundation.md) — How the vision aligns with Shariah principles — Amanah, 'Adl, Maslahah, Khilafah — and serves broader Islamic values. Use when explaining the Islamic scholarly and values grounding behind this vision.
- [Target Audience](./open-sharia-enterprise/target-audience.md) — Who Open Sharia Enterprise serves — developers, businesses and entrepreneurs, and individuals — and what each gives and gets. Use when identifying who a piece of work or messaging is aimed at within the OSE audience.
- [Success Vision: What the World Looks Like When We Succeed](./open-sharia-enterprise/success-vision.md) — A concrete picture of the technology landscape, business ecosystem, community impact, systemic change, and cultural shift when this vision succeeds. Use when you need a concrete picture of what success looks like at scale, beyond the founding problem statement.
- [How This Differs from Conventional Enterprise](./open-sharia-enterprise/how-this-differs-from-conventional-enterprise.md) — How Open Sharia Enterprise's open, ethical-profit model differs from conventional closed-system enterprise. Use when contrasting this project's business model with conventional proprietary enterprise.
- [Ethical Profit and the Islamic Enterprise Paradox](./open-sharia-enterprise/ethical-profit-and-the-paradox.md) — Why profit is halal and necessary when earned ethically, resolving the tension between Islamic values and profit-seeking. Use when explaining why profit and openness are not in tension here.
- [Measuring Success](./open-sharia-enterprise/measuring-success.md) — The adoption, impact, movement, and cultural indicators that show the vision is succeeding. Use when you need concrete, checkable indicators of whether this vision is actually succeeding.
- [Related Documentation (Open Sharia Enterprise Vision)](./open-sharia-enterprise/related-documentation.md) — Links from the ecosystem vision to the principles, conventions, development, agent, and workflow layers it inspires. Use when navigating from the ecosystem vision to the layers it governs.
