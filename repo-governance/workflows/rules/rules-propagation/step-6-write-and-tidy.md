# Step 6: Write and Subject-Scoped Tidy

The rule is written, and every other surface stating its subject is brought into agreement. The
sweep is bounded by the subject recorded at Step 2 — nothing wider.

## Write

Write the normalized statement into the destination decided at Step 4, matching the destination
document's existing voice, density, and structure. A rule that reads as a foreign insertion invites
a later editor to "clean it up" into something weaker.

## Consolidate the Subject

Enumerate every rule and discoverability surface stating the Step 2 subject — not only the ones the
conflict scan surfaced. Record its surviving canonical home and exactly one verdict:

| Verdict   | Apply when and action                                                           |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Keep      | Distinct, correctly placed; retain with a recorded rationale                    |
| Amend     | Partial, weak, or incoherent; align it without widening scope                   |
| Merge     | Fragmented or redundant; move its distinct substance into the canonical home    |
| Delete    | Fully redundant; remove it and preserve any necessary link or index path        |
| Relocate  | Correct obligation, wrong layer or surface; move it and leave needed navigation |
| Supersede | Replaced under Step 3; retire it and record the replacement                     |

Apply merge or delete only when the surviving canonical material preserves every distinct
obligation and necessary discoverability path. Fix the **class**, not the sites one search happened
to name. A known redundant or unnecessary statement may remain only with a recorded keep rationale;
an unexamined surface and an examined-and-kept surface are otherwise indistinguishable.
Write this complete inventory to the placement manifest.

## Reindex

Any file created, renamed, or deleted updates its parent index with a full annotation, and the
governance index above that. An index entry that lists a child without describing it fails the
completeness requirement, and a child missing from its index is unreachable.

## Regenerate

Where the write touched a source that mirrors elsewhere, regenerate the mirrors and let them land
in the same commit. Never hand-edit a mirror. Where the write touched a declaration, revalidate the
declaration's schema before moving on.

## Budget

Measure the word count of every file this step touched. A single added table row can cross a
ceiling, and the sanctioned remedy is always a split with an annotated index — never compression
that drops substance, and never a raised threshold. Before accepting the split, compare the
normalized rule and its surviving surfaces field by field: obligation, audience qualifier,
strength, scope, boundaries, exceptions, pass/violation conditions, and enforcement disposition
must retain the same meaning. “Shorter” is not success when any field weakened or disappeared.

## Ledger

Record every path on the file-touch ledger opened at Step 1, including deletions.
