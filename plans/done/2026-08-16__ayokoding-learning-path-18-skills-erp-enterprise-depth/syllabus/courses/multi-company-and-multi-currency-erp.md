# Multi-company and Multi-currency ERP

## Outcome

Multi-entity ERP requires clear legal-entity ownership and controlled currency translation.

## Prerequisites

- `record-to-report-systems`

## Format

By Example

## Concepts and examples

- State the governing business event and canonical record.
- Compare a normal path with a safe exception path.
- Reconcile the result to retained evidence and accountable approval.

## Practice and assessment

Explain the separate accounting roles of transaction, functional, and reporting currency.

A learner passes by explaining the policy, state transition, control, and evidence for a synthetic case.
