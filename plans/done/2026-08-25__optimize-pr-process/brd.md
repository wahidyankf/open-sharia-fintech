# Business Requirements: Optimize the Pull Request Process

## Goal and Rationale

Improve review quality without turning delivery into ceremony. Industry guidance favors small,
self-contained changes, descriptions that explain why and how to review, and comments that are
specific, respectful, and educational. Native GitHub conversations preserve that context for later
readers. [Web-cited] Evidence is mapped in
[README.md](./README.md#evidence-labels-and-sources); BR-1–BR-10 are user-ratified `[Judgment call]`
contracts, not measured outcomes.

## Affected Roles, Pains, and Benefits

| Role                              | Current pain                                                        | Expected benefit                                                   |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| PR author                         | Intent, scope, and review order are easy to bury in a long artifact | A short description tells a human what changed, why, and where     |
| Coding-bootcamp graduate engineer | Review terms can assume university-level theory or hidden repo lore | Plain explanations teach the evidence, impact, and safe next step  |
| Reviewer                          | A fixed specialist roster can repeat low-risk checks and miss risk  | A review route selects the smallest justified specialist set       |
| Fixer                             | Review can be treated as an order instead of a testable claim       | Four-way judgment makes fixes and reasoned pushback equally valid  |
| Maintainer                        | Merged history can hide scope growth or why a thread was resolved   | The PR itself preserves exact-head decisions, replies, and checks  |
| Plan executor                     | Large work can mix plans, rules, code, and cross-repo obligations   | Ordered, stable slices expose dependencies and rollback boundaries |

## Requirements

- **BR-1 — Human-readable PRs:** one cohesive outcome, plain-language context, deliberate reading
  order, review focus, verification, risk, integration safety, rollback, and concise non-goals.
- **BR-2 — Educational review:** findings explain impact, evidence, and a bounded remedy in language
  understandable to a coding-bootcamp graduate; optional teaching never masquerades as a blocker.
- **BR-3 — Critical fixing:** every unresolved finding receives one four-way disposition and a
  same-thread reply; valid pushback is expected, not treated as disobedience.
- **BR-4 — Bounded convergence:** prepare thoroughly for Cycle 1, batch verified fixes, target
  Cycles 1–3, recover autonomously through Cycle 5, and hard-stop before Cycle 6.
- **BR-5 — PR-native auditability:** bodies, reviews, replies, commits, checks, resolutions,
  obligations, deviations, and supersession links must make the process reconstructable.
- **BR-6 — Scope control:** review fixes address the cited defect class only; adjacent work becomes
  a linked follow-up. A review cycle never silently expands the PR.
- **BR-7 — Minimal machinery:** use prose and current GitHub/repository capabilities unless a
  measured gap proves a new mechanism necessary and cheaper than its maintenance burden.
- **BR-8 — Cross-repo coherence:** public portable rules propagate through the canonical workflow;
  private adapts from exact merged pins and records semantic correspondence or deviation. One stable
  defect-lineage ID follows a root cause across superseded or late repair pairs so relabeling cannot
  reset the one-reversal boundary.
- **BR-9 — Large-plan safety:** plan docs, rules, bindings, code, tests, ideas, and closure become
  sequential delivery units in one worktree per repo, with a stable `main` between units.
- **BR-10 — Trunk safety:** every multi-PR sequence names its lightest-fit “feature flag,” dependency,
  proof that `main` stays stable, and rollback in reverse dependency order.

## Observable Success

Success is evidenced in the resulting PR artifacts, not inferred from agent confidence:

- A cold reader can find outcome, reason, scope/non-goals, reading order, verification, risk,
  integration safety, and rollback without opening an agent transcript.
- Before specialist review begins, the PR contains a plain-language review-route record with exact base/head, diff
  scope, risk, specialist choices/skips, current evidence, settled history, and the changed probe.
- Blocking findings teach evidence, impact, remedy, and refutation; fixers reply in the same thread
  with one disposition and preserve `Generated by AI` on every AI-authored artifact.
- Ordinary work becomes merge-ready in Cycles 1–3. Cycles 4–5 show a changed recovery strategy;
  unresolved work stops before Cycle 6 without silently widening scope.
- Each large plan uses one worktree per repository, merges sequential unstacked PRs from current
  `main`, and leaves the trunk stable through a named dormant or reversible safety choice.
- Portable public rules reach private through the named propagation workflow and exact immutable
  pins; the private PR records that the obligation is satisfied or deliberately deviates with reason.

## Business Scope and Non-Goals

In scope are the human experience and governance of plan making, PR authoring, review routing,
review/fixer conversations, convergence, quality gates, large-plan slicing, cross-repo propagation,
idea retirement, and closure. The audience includes engineers without a computer-science degree.

This plan does not optimize for fewer comments, automatic agreement, maximum throughput, or an
academic level of detail. It does not add a review bot, merge queue, database, registry, parser,
universal feature-flag system, or mechanical enforcement unless later evidence proves the existing
human-readable process cannot meet a requirement.

## Business Risks and Responses

- **Process becomes paperwork:** keep PR bodies and reviews concise; link deep evidence instead of
  copying it into every artifact.
- **Educational prose hides the decision:** label optional teaching as nonblocking and lead every
  finding with the concrete claim and impact.
- **Review expands scope:** freeze the promised outcome at cycle entry; same-defect completeness is
  allowed, while unrelated improvements become follow-ups.
- **Automation creates maintenance burden:** require a demonstrated repeated gap, named owner,
  reversible design, and benefit greater than upkeep before adding machinery.
- **Cross-repo rules drift:** pin the merged public source, measure private destination state live,
  and record semantic correspondence or deviation in the private PR.
- **The hard stop leaves a blocker:** preserve the complete PR-native record and ask a human before
  Cycle 6 rather than claiming a false pass or continuing indefinitely.
