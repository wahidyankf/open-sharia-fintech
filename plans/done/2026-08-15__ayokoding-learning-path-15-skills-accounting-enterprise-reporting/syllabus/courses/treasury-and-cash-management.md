# Treasury and Cash Management (By Example)

**Course ID**: `treasury-and-cash-management` · **Scope note**: reasons about cash position and controlled settlement; banking integration and investment execution are excluded.

## Why this exists

A bank balance can agree with the book while timing, authorization, or concentration risk remains hidden.

## Prerequisites

- **Prior courses**: `accounts-payable-and-procure-to-pay`, `accounts-receivable-and-order-to-cash`.

## Accuracy notes

- [Verified — stable domain fact] Cash reconciliation needs bank evidence, book evidence, timing analysis, and controlled approval.

## Concepts

- **co-01 · cash-position** — evidence-backed available cash view.
- **co-02 · bank-reconciliation** — comparison of book and bank evidence.
- **co-03 · outstanding-item** — known timing difference.
- **co-04 · liquidity** — ability to settle obligations.
- **co-05 · authorization** — accountable payment decision.
- **co-06 · concentration** — dependence on one counterparty or account.
- **co-07 · forecast** — documented expected cash movement.
- **co-08 · silent-failure** — reconciled balances can conceal an unauthorized transfer.

## Worked examples

- **ex-01 · dated-reconciliation** — identify a timing difference.
- **ex-02 · cash-forecast** — explain sources and uses with uncertainty.
- **ex-03 · silent-failure-unauthorized-transfer** — investigate a balanced but unsupported movement.

Review a paper cash position and reconciliation; do not build a treasury platform.
