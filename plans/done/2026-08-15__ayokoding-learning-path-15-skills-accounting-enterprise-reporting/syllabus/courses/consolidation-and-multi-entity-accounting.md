# Consolidation and Multi-entity Accounting (By Example)

**Course ID**: `consolidation-and-multi-entity-accounting` · **Scope note**: explains paper consolidation judgment; group-reporting software is excluded.

## Why this exists

Group totals can add correctly while omitting elimination, ownership, or reporting-boundary facts.

## Prerequisites

- **Prior courses**: `chart-of-accounts-and-data-modeling`, `financial-statements-and-close-cycle`, `multi-currency-accounting-and-fx-translation`.

## Accuracy notes

- [Web-cited: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-10-consolidated-financial-statements/; accessed 2026-08-15] Terminology is paraphrased; examples are original.

## Concepts

- **co-01 · reporting-entity** — economic boundary of reports.
- **co-02 · control** — basis for consolidation judgment.
- **co-03 · component-ledger** — entity-level source records.
- **co-04 · ownership-interest** — documented claim in an entity.
- **co-05 · elimination** — removal of internal effects.
- **co-06 · intercompany-balance** — group-internal claim or obligation.
- **co-07 · consolidation-adjustment** — reviewable group-level change.
- **co-08 · silent-failure** — totals can foot while an internal sale remains twice counted.

## Worked examples

- **ex-01 · component-map** — identify the reporting boundary.
- **ex-02 · intercompany-elimination** — remove reciprocal internal effects.
- **ex-03 · silent-failure-uneliminated-sale** — find an added total that overstates group revenue.

## Applied synthesis (no build — A6)

Review a paper consolidation pack; do not implement consolidation software.
