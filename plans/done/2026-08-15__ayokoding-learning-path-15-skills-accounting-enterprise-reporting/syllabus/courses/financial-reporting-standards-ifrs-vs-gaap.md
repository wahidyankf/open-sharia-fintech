# Financial Reporting Standards: IFRS vs GAAP (Annotated-concept)

**Course ID**: `financial-reporting-standards-ifrs-vs-gaap` · **Scope note**: compares disclosed policy choices; legal advice and authoritative-text reproduction are excluded.

## Why this exists

Policy labels can look comparable while measurement, presentation, and disclosure judgments differ.

## Prerequisites

- **Prior courses**: `accrual-accounting-and-revenue-recognition`, `lease-and-intangible-asset-accounting`.

## Accuracy notes

- [Web-cited: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/; accessed 2026-08-15] This is a clean-room orientation, not a standards interpretation.

## Concepts

- **co-01 · reporting-framework** — governing reporting basis.
- **co-02 · accounting-policy** — chosen, documented treatment.
- **co-03 · recognition** — whether an item enters reports.
- **co-04 · measurement** — attributed amount basis.
- **co-05 · presentation** — statement placement.
- **co-06 · disclosure** — explanatory reporting context.
- **co-07 · comparability** — meaningful reading across entities or periods.
- **co-08 · silent-failure** — consistent labels can conceal incomparable policies.

## Worked examples

- **ex-01 · policy-register** — make a policy assumption explicit.
- **ex-02 · disclosure-comparison** — distinguish label from disclosed basis.
- **ex-03 · silent-failure-comparable-label** — identify a misleading like-for-like comparison.
