# Multi-currency Accounting and FX Translation (By Example)

**Course ID**: `multi-currency-accounting-and-fx-translation` · **Scope note**: translates documented currency exposures; treasury execution is excluded.

## Why this exists

Currency amounts can balance while using an obsolete rate or the wrong functional-currency basis. Preserve amount, currency, rate source, date, and translation purpose.

## Prerequisites

- **Prior courses**: `financial-statements-and-close-cycle`.

## Accuracy notes

- [Web-cited: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-21-the-effects-of-changes-in-foreign-exchange-rates/; accessed 2026-08-15] Original examples only; no standard clauses or illustrations reproduced.

## Concepts

- **co-01 · functional-currency** — primary economic environment basis.
- **co-02 · transaction-currency** — denomination of the event.
- **co-03 · rate-source** — accountable exchange-rate evidence.
- **co-04 · spot-rate** — rate at recognition date.
- **co-05 · monetary-item** — item settled in fixed currency units.
- **co-06 · translation** — conversion for reporting.
- **co-07 · exchange-difference** — change from rate movement.
- **co-08 · silent-failure** — equal debits and credits can use the wrong rate date.

## Worked examples

- **ex-01 · dated-invoice** — capture currency and source rate at recognition.
- **ex-02 · month-end-remeasurement** — identify an evidence-backed rate change.
- **ex-03 · silent-failure-stale-rate** — detect a balanced translation based on obsolete evidence.

Prepare a paper translation memo; do not build a foreign-exchange system.
