# Audit, Controls, and Compliance (Annotated-concept)

**Course ID**: `audit-controls-and-compliance` · **Scope note**: develops evidence-led control reasoning; audit execution and compliance tooling are excluded.

## Why this exists

A documented control can exist on paper while the underlying risk remains unmanaged.

## Prerequisites

- **Prior courses**: `financial-statements-and-close-cycle`.

## Accuracy notes

- [Web-cited: https://www.coso.org/guidance-on-ic; accessed 2026-08-15] COSO is contextual source material; examples are original.

## Concepts

- **co-01 · risk** — uncertainty affecting an objective.
- **co-02 · control-objective** — condition the control supports.
- **co-03 · control-activity** — accountable action addressing risk.
- **co-04 · segregation** — separation of conflicting duties.
- **co-05 · evidence** — reviewable proof of activity.
- **co-06 · exception** — documented deviation requiring disposition.
- **co-07 · monitoring** — recurring control-effectiveness observation.
- **co-08 · silent-failure** — a signed checklist can leave an untested risk unchanged.

## Worked examples

- **ex-01 · risk-control-map** — connect a risk to objective and evidence.
- **ex-02 · exception-review** — decide whether a deviation is resolved.
- **ex-03 · silent-failure-rubber-stamp** — detect approval without substantive review.
