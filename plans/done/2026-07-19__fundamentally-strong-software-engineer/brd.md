# Business Requirements — The Fundamentally Strong Software Engineer

## Business Goal

Grow ayokoding-www's value to **practising engineers** (not just first-time learners) by giving them
a fast, breadth-first way to refresh and self-test across the whole software-engineering field. This
broadens the site's audience beyond deep-dive learners to the much larger population of working
engineers doing periodic knowledge maintenance (interview prep, onboarding, design-review prep,
gap-closing). In an AI/LLM-assisted workflow this maintenance is strategic, not incidental: the
engineer who stays strong in the fundamentals is the one who can trust, verify, and override what
the tools produce.

**The success measure is an outcome, not content volume.** Per the user, the length of any topic or
of the whole tutorial is not a concern — the aim is that a reader genuinely becomes a **fundamentally strong
software engineer**: grounded broadly and deeply enough to operate at **any company size, at any level
of complexity, and at any career altitude from individual contributor up to CTO**. Every scope and
quality decision in this plan is judged against that grounding outcome, never against page count.

## Why Now

- The existing `learn/software-engineering/` tree is depth-optimized (Diátaxis, by-example,
  in-the-field). It serves _learning a topic_ well but not _reloading a topic fast_. [Repo-grounded —
  `apps/ayokoding-www/content/en/learn/software-engineering/overview.md`]
- "Relearn-then-drill" content has a distinct, recurring use pattern (work the examples, then test
  recall) that the existing scattered deep tutorials do not present as one breadth-first surface. A
  dedicated section captures that pattern with by-example depth per topic.
- **In the age of AI and LLMs, fundamentals matter more, not less.** When code is increasingly
  generated by assistants, an engineer's durable value is the _solid understanding_ needed to judge,
  review, and correct that output — knowing why a data structure, a concurrency model, an
  architecture, or a security control is right. This section exists to keep that grounding sharp: it
  is the human-in-the-loop's reference for staying fundamentally strong across the field rather than
  deferring blindly to generated answers.
- The content is **self-contained and additive** — zero risk to existing subtrees, so the downside
  of shipping it is low.

## Affected Roles

- **Working software engineers (primary readers)** — gain a single breadth-first refresh + drill
  surface across CS → IT security.
- **Interview candidates / career-switchers** — gain a compact review-and-test loop.
- **ayokoding-www content maintainers** — gain a new section to extend; uniform page anatomy keeps
  maintenance cost predictable.

Solo-maintainer repo — no sign-off/sponsor/stakeholder ceremony applies.

## Business-Level Success Signals

These are **observable signals**, not fabricated KPIs:

- [Judgment call] The section is _discoverable_: a reader landing on `learn/` (or the `learn/fundamentally-strong/` collection)
  sees "The Fundamentally Strong Software Engineer" in the nav and can reach any topic in ≤2 clicks.
- [Judgment call] The section is _usable as a learn-then-drill loop_: for every topic, a reader can
  work the learning subtree and then immediately practise the matching drilling page.
- **Coverage is complete and symmetric**: all 94 topics exist in BOTH tracks, in identical order
  (observable: topic/weight parity between `learning/` and `drilling/`).
- **By-example pace per topic**: each learning topic clears its applicable checker's density/format
  bands (`apps-ayokoding-www-by-example-checker` for code topics; `apps-ayokoding-www-general-checker`
  for concept topics). [Repo-grounded — agents exist in `.claude/agents/`]
- **Quality gates pass**: content clears the applicable maker's checker,
  `apps-ayokoding-www-facts-checker`, `apps-ayokoding-www-link-checker`, and markdown lint (observable:
  green checks). [Repo-grounded — agents exist in `.claude/agents/`]

## Business Scope — Non-Goals

- **Not** a replacement for the existing deep `learn/software-engineering/` content — it complements,
  never supersedes, and does not link into it.
- **Not** a certification, assessment-scoring, or progress-tracking product — drilling is static
  self-check, no server state.
- **Not** bilingual in this plan — Indonesian mirror is explicitly deferred.
- **Not** an app/UI feature — no code under `apps/ayokoding-www/src/`; markdown content only.

## Business Risks

| Risk                                                      | Likelihood | Mitigation                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Content duplicates existing deep tutorials, diluting both | Medium     | Self-contained section; breadth-first framing; own by-example subtree distinct from existing trees.                          |
| Breadth invites factual drift across 94 wide topics       | Medium     | `apps-ayokoding-www-facts-checker` pass on all pages before archival; pre-authoring `web-researcher` accuracy sweep (DD-28). |
| Two tracks drift out of topic-order sync                  | Low        | Fixed weight scheme (learning 101..194 / drilling 201..294); parity check in delivery gate.                                  |
| Breadth becomes shallow "grounded-in-name-only" coverage  | Medium     | Depth-to-mastery per topic is the done-bar (not length); checker quality floors; level-phased.                               |
