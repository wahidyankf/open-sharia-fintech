# Zakah Computation and Reporting for Systems (By Example)

**Course ID**: `zakah-computation-and-reporting-for-systems` · **Scope note**: models evidence-led Zakah reporting; legal rulings, tax filing, and production calculation services are excluded.

## Why this exists

Zakah is its own obligation under AAOIFI FAS 9, not income tax and not computed on the same base.

## Prerequisites

- **Prior courses**: `islamic-contract-modeling-for-systems`.

## Accuracy notes

- [Web-cited: https://aaoifi.com/financial-accounting-standards/?lang=en; accessed 2026-08-16] AAOIFI FAS 9 is named for context; no calculation rules or standards text are reproduced.

## Concepts

- **co-01 · zakah-obligation** — distinct reported obligation.
- **co-02 · asset-base** — documented base subject to review.
- **co-03 · reporting-period** — stated assessment period.
- **co-04 · eligibility-evidence** — reviewed input support.
- **co-05 · calculation-assumption** — disclosed model choice.
- **co-06 · payment-record** — settlement evidence.
- **co-07 · tax-boundary** — non-equivalence with income tax.
- **co-08 · silent-failure** — an arithmetically correct result can use the wrong base.

## Worked examples

- **ex-01 · asset-base-register** — distinguish supported inputs from assumptions.
- **ex-02 · obligation-and-payment** — separate calculation from settlement evidence.
- **ex-03 · silent-failure-tax-substitution** — detect folding Zakah into income tax.

## Applied synthesis (no build — A6)

Prepare a paper Zakah reporting memo; do not build a calculation service.
