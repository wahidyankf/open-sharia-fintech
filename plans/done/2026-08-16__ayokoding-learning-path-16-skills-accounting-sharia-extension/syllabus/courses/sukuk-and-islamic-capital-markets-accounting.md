# Sukuk and Islamic Capital Markets Accounting (Annotated-concept)

**Course ID**: `sukuk-and-islamic-capital-markets-accounting` · **Scope note**: compares disclosed Sukuk structures with bond treatment; investment advice and issuance execution are excluded.

## Why this exists
