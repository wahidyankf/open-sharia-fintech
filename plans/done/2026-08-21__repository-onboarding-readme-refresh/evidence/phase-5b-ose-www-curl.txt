## What the Phase 5B screenshots do and do not evidence

Added 2026-08-21, after review noted that the three phase-5b PNGs are sha256-identical to their
phase-5a counterparts.

That identity is the expected result, not a copy. Both phases were rendered by the same host
browser: Phase 5B was inspected through the container's published host loopback port, not from
inside the container. Both phases served an identical 38399-byte response body. Same renderer plus
identical bytes at identical viewports produces identical PNGs.

The consequence worth stating plainly is that the phase-5b images evidence that the container
served the page correctly. They do NOT evidence Ubuntu-side rendering, which the filenames could be
read to imply. The Ubuntu-specific proof of this phase is the in-container bootstrap and the
in-container curl recorded above, not the screenshots.

The images are kept rather than deduplicated, because the per-phase pairing is itself the record of
what each journey checked.
