# Accrual Accounting and Revenue Recognition (By Example)

**Course ID**: `accrual-accounting-and-revenue-recognition` · **Format**: By Example.

**Scope note**: Applies accrual and period-cutoff reasoning; contract-specific Sharia treatment is excluded.

## Why this exists · the big idea

- **The problem before the solution**: cash timing is not performance timing.
- **Keep-this-if-you-forget-everything**: recognize activity in the period it is earned or consumed, supported by evidence.

## Prerequisites

- **Prior courses**: `journal-entries-and-posting-mechanics`.
- **Assumed knowledge**: statement periods and documented entries.

## Accuracy notes

- [Web-cited: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/; accessed 2026-08-15] Examples restate concepts without clause text.

## Concepts

- **co-01 · accrual** — records an incurred effect before cash movement.
- **co-02 · deferral** — holds cash receipt or payment for later recognition.
- **co-03 · performance** — work or transfer supporting recognition.
- **co-04 · cutoff** — correct period boundary.
- **co-05 · receivable** — earned amount awaiting collection.
- **co-06 · contract-liability** — cash received before performance.
- **co-07 · estimate** — documented judgment with review.
- **co-08 · silent-failure** — early revenue can leave the trial balance perfectly balanced.

## Worked examples

### Beginner

- **ex-01 · prepaid-service** — defer a payment until consumption. (co-01, co-02)

### Advanced
