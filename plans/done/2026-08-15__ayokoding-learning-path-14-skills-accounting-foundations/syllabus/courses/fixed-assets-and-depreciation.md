# Fixed Assets and Depreciation (By Example)

**Course ID**: `fixed-assets-and-depreciation` · **Format**: By Example.

**Scope note**: Covers asset records, useful-life judgment, and depreciation review; lease classification is reserved for the final course.

## Why this exists · the big idea

- **The problem before the solution**: capitalizing a cost and choosing a useful life can both look reasonable while distorting performance.
- **Keep-this-if-you-forget-everything**: an asset balance needs existence, purpose, useful-life, and impairment evidence.

## Prerequisites

- **Prior courses**: `financial-statements-and-close-cycle`.
- **Assumed knowledge**: adjusting entries and statement classification.

## Accuracy notes

- [Web-cited: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-16-property-plant-and-equipment/; accessed 2026-08-15] Concepts are restated without clauses or examples.

## Concepts

- **co-01 · capitalization** — recognition of a qualifying long-lived resource.
- **co-02 · asset-register** — evidence-backed record of each asset.
- **co-03 · useful-life** — expected service period.
- **co-04 · residual-value** — expected value at end of use.
- **co-05 · depreciation** — systematic allocation of depreciable amount.
- **co-06 · disposal** — controlled retirement of an asset.
- **co-07 · impairment-indicator** — evidence requiring reconsideration of carrying value.
- **co-08 · silent-failure** — a smooth depreciation schedule can remain balanced after an asset ceases to provide benefit.

## Worked examples

### Beginner

- **ex-01 · asset-register-row** — record original cost, location, owner, and service date. (co-01, co-02)
