# Accounting Foundations (By Example)

**Course ID**: `accounting-foundations` · **Format**: By Example.

**Scope note**: Establishes the accounting equation, double-entry logic, and control vocabulary for a single entity. It excludes account classification design and statement preparation, which belong to the next two courses.

## Why this exists · the big idea

- **The problem before the solution**: a transaction record is not trustworthy merely because its debit and credit columns contain numbers.
- **Keep-this-if-you-forget-everything**: every entry changes the equation in a traceable way, and every trace needs a business explanation.

## Prerequisites

- **Prior courses**: none.
- **Assumed knowledge**: arithmetic with signed amounts.

## Accuracy notes

- [Verified — stable domain fact] Double-entry records equal debit and credit totals; examples are original and do not reproduce any chart of accounts or standards text.

## Concepts

- **co-01 · accounting-equation** — assets equal liabilities plus equity.
- **co-02 · dual-aspect** — one economic event has at least two accounting effects.
- **co-03 · debit-credit-direction** — debit and credit are side labels, not synonyms for increase and decrease.
- **co-04 · source-document** — evidence explains why a transaction exists.
- **co-05 · ledger-balance** — an account balance accumulates posted changes.
- **co-06 · trial-balance** — a control total checks arithmetic equality.
- **co-07 · entity-boundary** — owner and business activity remain distinct.
- **co-08 · forward-boundary** — account structure is owned by `chart-of-accounts-and-data-modeling`.

## Worked examples

### Beginner

- **ex-01 · owner-cash-contribution** — record an owner contribution and verify assets and equity rise equally. (co-01, co-02)

### Intermediate

- **ex-02 · paid-supplier-invoice** — classify a supplier payment and explain both account effects. (co-03, co-04)

### Advanced

- **ex-03 · balanced-but-unexplained** — reject a balanced entry without source evidence. (co-04, co-06)

## Applied synthesis (no build — A6)

Reconcile a five-transaction paper ledger to the accounting equation and annotate the evidence for each entry; do not implement a ledger.
