# Financial Statements and Close Cycle (By Example)

**Course ID**: `financial-statements-and-close-cycle` · **Format**: By Example.

**Scope note**: Connects a classified ledger to financial statements and a controlled period close for one entity. It excludes journal-entry mechanics and detailed recognition judgments, which follow in later courses.

## Why this exists · the big idea

- **The problem before the solution**: a ledger is operational data until a repeatable close turns it into an explainable reporting period.
- **Keep-this-if-you-forget-everything**: a close is a sequence of evidence-backed checks, not a button that makes numbers final.

## Prerequisites

- **Prior courses**: `chart-of-accounts-and-data-modeling`.
- **Assumed knowledge**: account classes, normal balances, and the accounting equation.

## Accuracy notes

- [Verified — stable domain fact] Financial statements summarize classified balances; examples use invented entities and amounts rather than standards illustrations.

## Concepts

- **co-01 · income-statement** — summarizes performance over a period.
- **co-02 · balance-sheet** — summarizes resources and claims at a date.
- **co-03 · cash-flow-view** — explains cash movement separately from profit.
- **co-04 · close-calendar** — assigns ownership and timing to close work.
- **co-05 · reconciliation** — compares an account to independent evidence.
- **co-06 · adjusting-entry** — aligns balances with the reporting period.
- **co-07 · review-threshold** — highlights changes requiring explanation.
- **co-08 · forward-boundary** — journal creation and posting controls are owned by `journal-entries-and-posting-mechanics`.

## Worked examples

### Beginner

- **ex-01 · three-statement-map** — map classified trial-balance accounts to statement sections. (co-01, co-02, co-03)

### Intermediate

- **ex-02 · close-calendar** — assign a preparer, reviewer, evidence source, and deadline to five close tasks. (co-04, co-05)

### Advanced

- **ex-03 · unexplained-margin-change** — identify a period change that demands investigation before close approval. (co-06, co-07)

## Applied synthesis (no build — A6)

Perform a paper close for a single entity: reconcile three balances, prepare draft statements, and write the review notes; do not build a reporting system.
