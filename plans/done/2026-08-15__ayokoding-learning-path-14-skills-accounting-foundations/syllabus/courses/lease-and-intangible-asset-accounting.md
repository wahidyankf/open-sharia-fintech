# Lease and Intangible Asset Accounting (By Example)

**Course ID**: `lease-and-intangible-asset-accounting` · **Format**: By Example.

**Scope note**: Introduces evidence-led classification and lifecycle review for leases and intangible resources; Sharia contract accounting is reserved for Plan 16.

## Why this exists · the big idea

- **The problem before the solution**: treating a long-lived commitment as a routine period cost can make leverage and performance look healthier than they are.
- **Keep-this-if-you-forget-everything**: classify the underlying right or resource from its economic substance, document judgment, and revisit it when facts change.

## Prerequisites

- **Prior courses**: `fixed-assets-and-depreciation`, `financial-statements-and-close-cycle`.
- **Assumed knowledge**: long-lived asset evidence and period close.

## Concepts

- **co-01 · right-of-use** — controlled use of an identified asset.
- **co-02 · lease-term** — enforceable use period with documented options.
- **co-03 · lease-obligation** — payment commitment view.
- **co-04 · intangible-resource** — identifiable non-physical resource.
- **co-05 · recognition-evidence** — support for classification judgment.
- **co-06 · amortization** — systematic allocation over useful period.
- **co-07 · reassessment-trigger** — fact change requiring review.
- **co-08 · silent-failure** — a balanced expense entry can conceal an omitted long-term obligation.
