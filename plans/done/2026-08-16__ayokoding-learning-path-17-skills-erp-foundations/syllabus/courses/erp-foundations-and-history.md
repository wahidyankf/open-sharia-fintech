**Course ID**: `erp-foundations-and-history` · **Format**: Annotated-Concept.

**Scope note**: Explains integrated enterprise records and their boundaries; it excludes data modelling and implementation selection. License-aware.

**Short summary**: ERP is a coordinated record of enterprise events, not a vendor label.

## Why this exists · the big idea

- **The problem before the solution**: isolated records create inconsistent operational truth.
- **Keep-this-if-you-forget-everything**: integration is a data and control problem before it is a product problem.

## Concepts

- **co-01 · enterprise-event** — a business occurrence represented once with accountable evidence.
- **co-02 · shared-record** — a fact reusable by more than one operational module.
- **co-03 · process-boundary** — an explicit handoff between accountable activities.
- **co-04 · master-data** — relatively durable shared business identity data.
- **co-05 · transaction-data** — time-bound evidence of an operational event.
- **co-06 · integration-debt** — reconciliation work caused by disconnected records.
- **co-07 · system-of-record** — the accountable source for a specific fact.
- **co-08 · license-boundary** — a clean-room description never reproduces proprietary structures.
