# ERP Fiscal Calendar and Period Close (Annotated-Concept)

**Course ID**: `erp-fiscal-calendar-and-period-close` · **Format**: Annotated-Concept.

**Scope note**: Defines fiscal periods, close controls, and reopening governance; it excludes reporting-standard measurement. License-aware.

**Short summary**: Period closure turns mutable operating facts into governed reporting evidence.

## Why this exists · the big idea

- **The problem before the solution**: unrestricted backdating rewrites reported history.
- **Keep-this-if-you-forget-everything**: a closed period needs a governed correction path, not silent edits.

## Prerequisites

- **Prior courses**: `erp-subledger-to-gl-architecture`.
- **Assumed knowledge**: accounting periods.
