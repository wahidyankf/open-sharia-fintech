# Payroll and Tax Accounting Essentials (By Example)

**Course ID**: `payroll-and-tax-accounting-essentials` · **Scope note**: records evidence-led payroll obligations; payroll administration and jurisdiction-specific advice are excluded.

## Why this exists

Gross-to-net calculations can add up while a classification, withholding, or remittance date is wrong.

## Prerequisites

- **Prior courses**: `chart-of-accounts-and-data-modeling`.

## Accuracy notes

- [Verified — stable domain fact] Payroll records require approved employment, calculation, payment, and remittance evidence; no jurisdictional rates are taught.

## Concepts

- **co-01 · gross-pay** — approved compensation before deductions.
- **co-02 · deduction** — authorized reduction.
- **co-03 · withholding** — amount retained for remittance.
- **co-04 · employer-charge** — employer-side obligation.
- **co-05 · payroll-liability** — amount owed after payroll.
- **co-06 · remittance** — controlled settlement of liability.
- **co-07 · worker-classification** — documented basis for treatment.
- **co-08 · silent-failure** — correct arithmetic can retain the wrong classification.

## Worked examples

- **ex-01 · approved-payroll-input** — trace authorization to gross pay.
- **ex-02 · payable-and-remittance** — distinguish pay date from liability settlement.
- **ex-03 · silent-failure-misclassification** — identify a balanced but unsupported treatment.

## Applied synthesis (no build — A6)

Prepare a paper payroll reconciliation; do not build a payroll service.
