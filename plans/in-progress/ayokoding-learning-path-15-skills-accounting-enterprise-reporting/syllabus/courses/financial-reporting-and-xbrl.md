# Financial Reporting and XBRL (Annotated-concept)

**Course ID**: `financial-reporting-and-xbrl` · **Scope note**: teaches report-to-taxonomy evidence and review; filing-platform construction is excluded.

## Why this exists

Digitally tagged reports can validate syntactically while a concept, period, unit, or extension tells the wrong business story.

## Prerequisites

- **Prior courses**: `financial-reporting-standards-ifrs-vs-gaap`.

## Accuracy notes

- [Web-cited: https://www.xbrl.org/the-standard/what/an-introduction-to-xbrl/; accessed 2026-08-15] No taxonomy content or filing examples are copied.

## Concepts

- **co-01 · taxonomy** — controlled reporting vocabulary.
- **co-02 · fact** — tagged reported value or disclosure.
- **co-03 · concept** — taxonomy meaning selected for a fact.
- **co-04 · context** — entity and period meaning.
- **co-05 · unit** — measure associated with a value.
- **co-06 · extension** — entity-specific disclosed concept.
- **co-07 · validation** — structural and business-rule review.
- **co-08 · silent-failure** — valid tags can assign a value to the wrong context.

## Worked examples

- **ex-01 · tagged-fact-card** — record concept, context, unit, and source.
- **ex-02 · extension-review** — justify a narrow extension.
- **ex-03 · silent-failure-wrong-context** — find a plausible value reported for the wrong period.
