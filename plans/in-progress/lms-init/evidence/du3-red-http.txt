# DU3-142 RED — HTTP step bindings exist, controllers do not

Per-feature results from apps/ose-lms-be/build/test-results/test/ (Gradle JUnit XML):

- LMS BE listener port resolution: tests=3 failures=0 errors=0
- LMS BE Actuator exposure: tests=2 failures=1 errors=0
- LMS BE health endpoint: tests=1 failures=1 errors=0
- LMS BE hello endpoint: tests=1 failures=1 errors=0

Failures, with the asserted and observed status:

- Actuator health endpoint reports the service is up: org.opentest4j.AssertionFailedError: expected: &lt;200&gt; but was: &lt;404&gt;
- Health endpoint returns a healthy status: org.opentest4j.AssertionFailedError: expected: &lt;200&gt; but was: &lt;404&gt;
- Hello endpoint returns the greeting: org.opentest4j.AssertionFailedError: expected: &lt;200&gt; but was: &lt;404&gt;

The health and hello scenarios fail with 404 because HealthController and HelloController
do not exist. The Actuator health scenario also fails with 404 because
spring-boot-starter-actuator is absent; that is the DU3-145 RED, observed here in the same
run. 'Actuator exposes no endpoint other than health' already passes, because with no
Actuator on the classpath /actuator/env is 404 for the trivial reason.
