# Accounts Payable and Procure to Pay (By Example)

**Course ID**: `accounts-payable-and-procure-to-pay` · **Format**: By Example.

**Scope note**: Covers invoice-to-payment controls; purchasing-system construction is excluded.

## Why this exists · the big idea

- **The problem before the solution**: paying a legitimate invoice twice can still produce balanced books.
- **Keep-this-if-you-forget-everything**: payment follows a three-way evidence match, not urgency alone.

## Prerequisites

- **Prior courses**: `journal-entries-and-posting-mechanics`, `accrual-accounting-and-revenue-recognition`.
- **Assumed knowledge**: supplier entries and period cutoff.

## Accuracy notes

- [Verified — stable domain fact] Procurement control separates request, receipt, invoice, and payment evidence.

## Concepts

- **co-01 · purchase-request** — authorized demand.
- **co-02 · purchase-order** — approved commitment.
- **co-03 · receipt** — evidence of goods or service.
- **co-04 · invoice-match** — comparison of obligation evidence.
- **co-05 · payable** — obligation awaiting settlement.
- **co-06 · payment-run** — controlled payment selection.
- **co-07 · vendor-master** — governed supplier identity.
- **co-08 · silent-failure** — duplicate payment can balance while consuming cash twice.

## Worked examples

### Beginner

- **ex-01 · three-way-match** — compare order, receipt, and invoice. (co-02–co-04)

### Intermediate

- **ex-02 · aged-payable-review** — prioritize a payment run with evidence. (co-05, co-06)

### Advanced

- **ex-03 · silent-failure-duplicate-invoice** — find duplicate settlement risk. (co-07, co-08)
