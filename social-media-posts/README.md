# OSE social media posts

This directory is the source archive for LinkedIn updates about Open Sharia
Enterprise: what the product is learning, what has changed, and why it matters.
It helps product-minded readers follow progress without needing to sift through
implementation detail. 📣

These files are publishing material, not a product onboarding route. To explore
the platform, start with the [root README](../README.md) and the
[OSE public getting-started tutorial](../docs/tutorials/getting-started-with-ose-public.md).

## Find a post

    social-media-posts/
    └── linkedin/
        └── YYYY/
            └── YYYY-MM-DD__linkedin__ose-update-<topic>.md

Posts live under the year in their filename’s publication date. The filename
also states the platform, content family, and topic, which keeps the archive
easy to scan and stable to link to.

## Write with a reader in mind

A good update is a small story, not a changelog. It should:

- open with the product or reader value;
- explain technical work in plain language when it matters;
- distinguish an experiment or pre-alpha direction from a shipped capability;
- use specific, supportable facts and links; and
- invite conversation without promising support, access, or external
  contribution routes that the project does not offer.

Before publishing, check factual accuracy, links, readability on a phone, and
LinkedIn’s current character limit. Do not include credentials, internal
operational detail, or private repository information.

## Status and historical records

Some published posts use historical repository names. They remain unchanged so
the archive does not rewrite history; GitHub redirects preserve the linked
locations where applicable. New posts should use the current repository names
and public product language.
