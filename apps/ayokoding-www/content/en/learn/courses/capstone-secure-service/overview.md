Take a prior HTTP service through the complete security loop: OWASP Top 10 (2025) mitigation, correct
identity boundaries, lab-local validation, detection, and a before/after posture record. It integrates
prior security and backend courses; it does not replace them.

## Safety boundary

Validation is performed only against a self-owned local service and synthetic logs. Keep secrets in local
environment configuration, never in source or telemetry fixtures.
