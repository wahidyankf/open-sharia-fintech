## Why this exists

A bill of materials and routing make a production result traceable to a versioned component structure and ordered operation path. They preserve the evidence future planning and quality flows need.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

Material consumption can reconcile while using a retired structure version. The observable signal is a production trace that cannot show the effective BOM and routing version.
