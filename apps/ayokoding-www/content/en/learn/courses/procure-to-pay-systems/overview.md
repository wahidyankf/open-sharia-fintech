## Why this exists

Procure to pay joins requisition, approved commitment, receipt, supplier invoice, payable, and settlement. A three-way match prevents payment from becoming detached from operational evidence.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

A payable can settle correctly while duplicating an already accepted supplier invoice. The observable signal is an invoice identity match showing multiple payables or payment proposals.
