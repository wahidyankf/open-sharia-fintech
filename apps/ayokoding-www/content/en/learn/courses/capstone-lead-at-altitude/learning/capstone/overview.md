1. Reuse the preceding service's golden signals and define an SLI, SLO, error budget, and symptom alert.
2. Seed a local violation and capture the alert, dashboard reading, and restoration decision.
3. Write a one-page strategy: service reliability investments → user or business outcomes → leading indicators.
4. Write a prioritization record that names the rejected work and error-budget trade-off.
5. Retrospect every programme pass: P0 editor foundations, P1 programming, P2 systems, P3 delivery,
   P4 internals, and P5 leadership—each with a concrete capability and sustaining team practice.

```text
If error-budget burn is high, reliability work outranks feature expansion until the service is stable.
```

An altitude view makes the constraint legible: strategy does not erase operational evidence, and a
retrospective must show what the team will repeat rather than simply list courses completed.
