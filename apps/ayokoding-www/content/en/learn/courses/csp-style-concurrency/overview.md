CSP-style Go concurrency transfers ownership through channels, bounds work, and makes cancellation explicit.

## Prerequisites

- [Just Enough Go](../just-enough-go/learning/overview.md)
- [Concurrency and Parallelism](../concurrency-and-parallelism/learning/overview.md)

## Scope boundary

This course teaches Go CSP pipelines and contrasts them with actor mailboxes; it does not re-teach Go syntax.
