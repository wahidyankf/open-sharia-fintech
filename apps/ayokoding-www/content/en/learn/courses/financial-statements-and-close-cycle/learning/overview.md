## By Example

1. **Statement map**: map trial-balance accounts into income statement, balance sheet, and cash-flow views.
2. **Close calendar**: assign owner, evidence, review, and deadline to close activities.
3. **Margin review**: investigate an unexplained period change before approval.

The applied exercise is a paper close for one entity. Do not build reporting or close software in this course.
