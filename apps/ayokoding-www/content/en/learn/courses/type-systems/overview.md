This course assumes [Functional Programming](../functional-programming/learning/overview.md),
[Programming Paradigms](../programming-paradigms/learning/overview.md), and
[Just Enough TypeScript](../just-enough-typescript/learning/overview.md). It deliberately does not
require Just Enough F#; the ML-family material begins from its own OCaml/Haskell baseline.

## Scope boundary

Learn algebraic types, inference, exhaustive matching, modules/typeclasses, and practical
map/bind pipelines. This is a working type-system course, not a formal proof-theory survey or a
language-specific standard library reference.
