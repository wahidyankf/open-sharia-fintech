## Why this exists

Cost information should illuminate a decision, not merely foot to a plausible total. Direct, indirect, fixed, and variable views carry different assumptions.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

A total can reconcile while an arbitrary allocation driver makes a product or service appear profitable when it is not. The observable signal is a material decision change when a documented alternative driver is used.
