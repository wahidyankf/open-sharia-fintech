## Why this exists

Sukuk reporting needs an instrument model tied to its contractual assets and holder reporting, not a relabelled conventional bond ledger. AAOIFI FAS 32, FAS 33, and FAS 34 provide the accounting reference points; the system must preserve the chosen framework, asset linkage, and currency evidence.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

Coupon-like cash movements can balance in a bond-shaped ledger while holder rights and assets are missing. The observable signal is a reporting record that cannot connect an investor balance to the instrument's documented asset or contract model.
