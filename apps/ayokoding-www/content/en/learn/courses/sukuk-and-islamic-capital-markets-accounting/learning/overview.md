## Annotated concepts

1. Record the contractual asset or service relationship behind the instrument.
2. Keep AAOIFI FAS 32, FAS 33, and FAS 34 references as citations, not reproduced text.
3. Separate holder reporting from a conventional bond abstraction.
4. Translate currency evidence without losing the instrument identity.

Annotate a reporting record; do not implement a trading venue.
