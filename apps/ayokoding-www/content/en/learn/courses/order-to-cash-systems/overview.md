## Why this exists

Order to cash connects an approved customer order, allocation, fulfilment evidence, invoice, receivable, and collection. Its control is the traceable relationship between a claim and what was fulfilled.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

A receivable can reconcile while the invoice is not supported by shipment evidence. The observable signal is a claim that cannot trace to a fulfilled quantity or authorized exception.
