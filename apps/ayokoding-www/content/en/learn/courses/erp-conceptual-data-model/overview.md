## Why this exists

An ERP conceptual model gives parties, items, documents, and events durable identities before modules choose their physical storage. It makes operational facts linkable without turning every consumer into an owner.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

Records can link successfully while one document line refers to a reused identity. The observable signal is an event trace that resolves to two incompatible business meanings.
