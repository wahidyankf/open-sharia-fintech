## Why this exists

Record the currency, rate source, date, and translation purpose behind each exposure.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

Equal debits and credits can use an obsolete rate or the wrong rate date. The observable signal is a rate-source record inconsistent with the event or reporting date.
