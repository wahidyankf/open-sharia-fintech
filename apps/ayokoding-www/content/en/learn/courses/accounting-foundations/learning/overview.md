## By Example

1. **Owner contribution**: cash rises with owner equity; verify both sides of the equation change equally.
2. **Supplier payment**: classify the event using its evidence, not the direction of cash alone.
3. **Balanced but unexplained**: reject an entry whose debit and credit agree but lacks a source document.

The applied exercise is a hand-reconciled five-event ledger. Do not build a ledger application in this course.
