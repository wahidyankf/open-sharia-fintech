## Run order

1. Run the timed [coding round](./coding-round.md), recording the clarification, plan, complexity,
   implementation, and verification evidence for two familiar patterns.
2. Complete the clean-checkout [take-home and live round](./take-home-and-live-round.md), keeping
   each narrated checkpoint green.
3. Present the [system-design walkthrough](./system-design-walkthrough.md) aloud, including every
   spine step, one deep dive, one bottleneck, and two trade-offs.
4. Deliver the [behavioral mock round](./behavioral-mock-round.md) with six true STAR answers,
   including a calm layoff or employment-gap probe if it applies to your history.
5. Complete the [score sheet](./score-sheet.md). Rate every round and select one next action rather
   than making a broad promise to "practice more."

Use realistic breaks, do not consult model answers during a timed segment, and preserve the exact
artifacts you produced. A missed test, unfinished explanation, or awkward transition is useful
evidence for the diagnosis.
