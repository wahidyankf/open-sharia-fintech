Use the complete form in [`scoresheet.md`](../scoresheet.md) immediately after the mock loop. Rate
every round from 1 to 4, cite the artifact that supports the rating, and choose the single lowest-
scoring dimension. Turn it into a next action with a prompt, timer, and evidence of completion.

The score is not an offer prediction. It is a repeatable diagnosis that lets the next rehearsal be
smaller and more specific.
