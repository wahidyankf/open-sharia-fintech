## Timed artifact

Run the six prompts in [`behavioral/mock-round.md`](../behavioral/mock-round.md), with two to three
minutes per answer plus follow-ups. Replace the fictional placeholders with true experiences from
your story bank; do not invent outcomes. Every answer must make Situation, Task, Action, Result, and
reflection audible.

The final prompt is a career-context probe. If a layoff or employment gap is relevant, give a calm,
brief, factual, forward-looking answer. If it is not, rehearse a truthful boundary rather than
borrowing a story. The goal is the honest professional communication already taught in [Behavioral &
Leadership Interviews](/en/learn/courses/behavioral-and-leadership-interviews).

Mark every answer on the score sheet before moving on. A fluent answer without an observable result
or reflection still needs repair.
