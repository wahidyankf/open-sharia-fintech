## Prerequisites

Complete [Coding Interview](/en/learn/courses/coding-interview), [Take-Home & Live
Coding](/en/learn/courses/take-home-and-live-coding), [System Design
Interview](/en/learn/courses/system-design-interview), and [Behavioral & Leadership
Interviews](/en/learn/courses/behavioral-and-leadership-interviews) first. This milestone assumes
their round techniques and rubrics; it adds no separate topic to learn.

You need a timer, Python 3 with `pytest`, a terminal, a plain editor without autocomplete during
the timed portions, a place to draw the system-design diagram, and a truthful story bank.

## Goal

Run one complete, self-administered interview loop: a coding round, a scoped take-home followed by
a narrated live round, a system-design walkthrough, and a behavioral mock. Preserve the artifacts,
then score the whole day. The handoffs matter: a strong isolated round is not yet evidence that you
can keep your communication, composure, and judgment consistent as the day gets longer.

## Scope boundary

This is an integration milestone, not a fifth interview-technique course. Return to the four
prerequisite courses to rehearse a particular pattern, delivery practice, design rubric, or STAR
answer. Here, use those established tools under realistic sequencing and diagnose one highest-
leverage improvement.

Start with [Learning](/en/learn/courses/capstone-interview-loop/learning/overview).
