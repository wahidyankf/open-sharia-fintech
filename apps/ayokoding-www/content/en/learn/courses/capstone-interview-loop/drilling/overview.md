Rehearse the loop at least once with a timer and an observer when possible. The five sections are
ordered to mirror the pressure of the capstone, not to reteach their prerequisite courses.

## Recall Q&A

1. What evidence makes a coding round complete? A stated contract, chosen pattern and invariant,
   complexity, implementation, and a verification result.
2. What makes a take-home reviewable from a clean checkout? An exact README command, focused tests,
   a small scope, and documented trade-offs.
3. What must a system-design interview answer contain? The full round spine, one deep dive, a named
   bottleneck, and explicit trade-offs.
4. What makes a behavioral answer credible? A truthful first-person STAR account with a result and
   reflection.
5. What is the capstone output? A score for every round and one concrete next action for the weakest
   round.

## Calculation practice

1. Allocate a realistic time budget for two coding prompts, implementation tests, a take-home review,
   a design walkthrough, and six behavioral answers. Reserve a transition break between each round.
2. For each coding prompt, state time and extra-space complexity before coding; compare your stated
   cost with the tests you run.
3. Set a two-minute budget per STAR answer and calculate whether six answers plus follow-ups fit the
   scheduled behavioral round.

## Scenario judgment

1. A coding test fails late. Decide whether to reduce scope, fix the invariant, or explain the
   remaining risk; keep the reasoning audible.
2. A take-home requirement is ambiguous. Write the clarification and the smallest safe assumption
   before changing code.
3. A design interviewer changes the scale constraint. Revisit the bottleneck and trade-offs rather
   than restarting the whole explanation.
4. A behavioral follow-up asks about an employment gap or layoff. Give only a truthful, bounded,
   forward-looking answer that you are comfortable sharing.

## Design exercise

Make a one-page loop card: round order, timer budgets, recovery phrases, artifact locations, and the
single rubric dimension you will watch in each round. Use it in a mock loop, then revise only the
field that was not usable under time pressure.

## Automaticity checklist

- [ ] I state a plan and complexity before coding.
- [ ] I can leave a take-home with a clean README and green focused tests.
- [ ] I can narrate the design spine, a bottleneck, and two trade-offs without losing the thread.
- [ ] I can tell six truthful STAR stories and handle a bounded career-context probe.
- [ ] I preserve artifacts before scoring myself.
- [ ] I select one observable repair for the weakest round.
