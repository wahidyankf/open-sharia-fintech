# Behavioral mock round

Use six true stories from your bank. The structure below is deliberately a record template rather
than a script: replace every bracket with accurate details and keep confidential names generalized.
Time each answer at two to three minutes, then note the follow-up evidence.

## Answer 1 · Ownership under pressure

- **Situation:** [specific project and stake].
- **Task:** [your responsibility].
- **Action:** [the first-person decision and collaboration].
- **Result:** [observable outcome].
- **Reflection:** [what you now do differently].

## Answer 2 · Disagreement and resolution

- **Situation:** [genuine disagreement and each perspective].
- **Task:** [decision or alignment needed].
- **Action:** [how you listened, tested, or reframed].
- **Result:** [decision and its evidence].
- **Reflection:** [how that changes your next disagreement].

## Answer 3 · Leadership without authority

- **Situation:** [cross-team or ambiguous context].
- **Task:** [the outcome that needed influence].
- **Action:** [the mechanism: framing, data, mentoring, or trust].
- **Result:** [observable change].
- **Reflection:** [what you would calibrate next time].

## Answer 4 · Failure and recovery

- **Situation:** [a real missed expectation or defect].
- **Task:** [what you owned after discovering it].
- **Action:** [how you communicated, corrected, and prevented recurrence].
- **Result:** [recovery evidence].
- **Reflection:** [the changed practice].

## Answer 5 · Judgment under ambiguity

- **Situation:** [incomplete information or competing priorities].
- **Task:** [the bounded decision].
- **Action:** [how you reduced uncertainty and named a trade-off].
- **Result:** [what happened and what remained uncertain].
- **Reflection:** [the next signal you would seek].

## Answer 6 · Career context: layoff or employment gap

- **Situation:** [only a truthful, shareable fact about the layoff, employment gap, or re-entry].
- **Task:** [what you needed to do next].
- **Action:** [bounded preparation, learning, care, or job-search activity without oversharing].
- **Result:** [present readiness and a concrete current capability].
- **Reflection:** [why this role and working context now fit].

If this probe is not part of your history, say so plainly and maintain a truthful boundary; do not
invent a layoff or employment gap. Score each answer for structure, ownership, specificity, result,
and reflection in [`scoresheet.md`](../scoresheet.md).
