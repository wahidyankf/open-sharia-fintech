## Why this exists

Quantity and price variance, cancellation, return, dispute, and partial fulfilment are normal operational states. Each requires an accountable owner and compensating evidence rather than an off-system workaround.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

An exception can be netted away while the original commitment remains falsely complete. The observable signal is a residual quantity or dispute state without a final decision owner.
