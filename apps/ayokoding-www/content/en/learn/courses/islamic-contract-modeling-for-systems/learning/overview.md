## By example

1. Model asset acquisition separately from the customer sale.
2. Capture the agreed markup at the sale event.
3. Post a sale receivable and revenue, not interest income.
4. Compare the evidence with an amortising loan schedule that has similar cash flows.

Produce an event model and review trail; do not build a financing product.
