## Why this exists

A murabaha is modelled as a trade with an underlying asset, fixed disclosed markup at sale, and a sale receivable. Its schedule can resemble a conventional amortising loan numerically, but its records must retain the asset, sale, receivable, and revenue evidence rather than label the return interest income. AAOIFI, PSAK Syariah, and MFRS with the Bank Negara Malaysia Shariah Governance Policy are coexisting models; AAOIFI is not the single Sharia accounting standard. Malaysia is not on AAOIFI's mandatory-adoption list, and Indonesia uses AAOIFI as a basis rather than adopting it.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

An amortisation table can balance while the source event is stored as a cash loan. The observable signal is the absence of an asset acquisition and disclosed-sale record for a purported murabaha.
