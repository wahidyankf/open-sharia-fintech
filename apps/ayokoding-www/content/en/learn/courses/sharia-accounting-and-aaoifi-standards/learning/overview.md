## Annotated concepts

1. Declare the reporting jurisdiction before choosing a policy.
2. Separate AAOIFI Financial Accounting Standards from Shari'ah standards.
3. Treat PSAK Syariah and MFRS-plus-BNM as coexisting models, not aliases.
4. Keep the unresolved doctrinal derivation outside software assertions.

Write a framework-selection memo with sources; do not encode a universal standards switch.
