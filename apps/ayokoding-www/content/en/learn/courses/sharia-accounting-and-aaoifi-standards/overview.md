## Why this exists

System builders need to name the reporting model before encoding an accounting policy. AAOIFI, PSAK Syariah, and MFRS with the Bank Negara Malaysia Shariah Governance Policy are three structurally different, coexisting models; AAOIFI is not the single Sharia accounting standard. Malaysia is not on AAOIFI's mandatory-adoption list; Indonesia uses AAOIFI as a basis rather than adopting it. This course states a practical consequence for transaction modelling without claiming that OI-2's doctrinal derivation is settled.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

A balanced posting can cite the wrong governing model. The observable signal is a policy-to-jurisdiction review that cannot trace the selected treatment to the entity's declared framework.
