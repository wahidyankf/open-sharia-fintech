## Prerequisites

- **Prior topics**: [Frontend Essentials](../frontend-essentials/learning/overview.md) and
  [Advanced Frontend](../advanced-frontend/learning/overview.md).
- **Tools**: a local static server, browser DevTools or Lighthouse, and structured-data validation
  tooling. All worked artifacts are local HTML, XML, text, or decision documents.

## Why this exists

A public page must be legible to readers, crawlers, and assistive technology at the same time. Clear
taxonomy, stable URLs, semantic HTML, honest metadata, and explicit structured data make that shared
structure visible; keyword tricks do not replace it.

This course starts with information architecture and semantic markup, then moves through crawl control,
sitemaps, canonicalization, structured data, internationalization, redirects, performance signals, and
measurement. It is intentionally about public discoverable content; an authenticated internal surface
still benefits from good information architecture but does not need search-engine machinery.
