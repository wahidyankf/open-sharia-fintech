## Why this exists

An audit trail preserves the actor, time, before-and-after values, reason, and correlation of material ERP changes. It is the Dangerous 1 boundary: a current value is insufficient without accountable history.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

An updated document can look valid while its reason and actor were never recorded. The observable signal is an audit event that lacks before-and-after evidence or a resolvable correlation id.
