## Why this exists

Tagging links a report fact to its concept, context, unit, and evidence; syntax alone is not enough.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

A structurally valid tag can assign a value to the wrong period. The observable signal is a fact context inconsistent with its source report.
