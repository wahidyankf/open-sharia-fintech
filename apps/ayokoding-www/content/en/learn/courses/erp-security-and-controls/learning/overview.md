## Annotated concepts

1. Apply least privilege, segregation of duties, access review, and change logging.
2. Identify the source-of-truth record and its accountable owner.
3. Preserve an evidence trail for exceptions rather than overwriting the result.

## Practice

Work from a small synthetic transaction set. State each assumption and the control that would detect a contradiction.
