This course assumes [Just Enough F#](../just-enough-fsharp/overview.md),
[Type Systems](../type-systems/overview.md), and
[Computer Science Foundations](../computer-science-foundations/learning/overview.md). It deliberately
does not require data-structures-and-algorithms-essentials.

Learn a compact language-processing pipeline: lexer, parser, AST, semantic analysis, interpreter,
and transpiler. The goal is inspectable implementation choices, not production compiler coverage.

## Sources

- [F# language reference](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/fsharp/language-reference/)
- [FParsec documentation](https://www.quanttec.com/fparsec/)
- [Crafting Interpreters](https://craftinginterpreters.com/) for a readable implementation-oriented reference.

All examples are original course artifacts.
