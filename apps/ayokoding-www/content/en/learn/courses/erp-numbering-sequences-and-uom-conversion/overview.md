## Why this exists

Identifiers must be unique in a defined scope, and quantities must retain their unit, conversion factor, rounding policy, and base-unit evidence. Formatting alone cannot provide either control.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

Converted inventory quantities can reconcile while a stale factor changes the physical meaning. The observable signal is a conversion audit whose factor or effective date cannot be reproduced.
