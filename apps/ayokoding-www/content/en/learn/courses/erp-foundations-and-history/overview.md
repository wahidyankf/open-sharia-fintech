## Why this exists

ERP is an integrated record of enterprise events, not a vendor label. It coordinates shared master data, operational transactions, controls, and evidence across accountable process boundaries.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

Two systems can each report plausible totals while disagreeing about the same customer or order. The observable signal is a reconciliation that cannot identify one accountable system of record.
