## By example

1. Version a policy selection with its jurisdiction and effective date.
2. Link each posting to an original contract event and evidence bundle.
3. Enforce balanced, idempotent entries and explicit reversals.
4. Separate operational views from reporting and audit views.

Design a ledger boundary and review query; do not build the retired capstone ledger.
