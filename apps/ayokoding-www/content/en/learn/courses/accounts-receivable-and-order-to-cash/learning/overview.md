## By Example

1. **Billed delivery**: connect fulfillment evidence to a customer claim.
2. **Partial cash application**: apply cash without hiding the remaining receivable.
3. **Aged balance**: investigate a mechanically valid balance whose collection evidence has gone stale.

Prepare a paper aging review and collection actions; do not construct an order-to-cash system.
