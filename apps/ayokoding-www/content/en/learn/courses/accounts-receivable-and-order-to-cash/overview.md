## Why this exists

Receivables require a trace from agreed obligation through fulfillment, billing, collection, or documented follow-up. An old balance can remain balanced while overstating collectible value.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

An aged receivable can leave the ledger balanced while overstating collectible value. The observable signal is overdue aging without a credible collection follow-up, dispute resolution, or subsequent cash receipt.
