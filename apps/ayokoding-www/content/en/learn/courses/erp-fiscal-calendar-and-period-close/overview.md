## Why this exists

A fiscal calendar gives business events reporting periods, while close controls prevent silent mutation of reported history. Late corrections need governed adjustments or reopening evidence.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

An adjustment can balance while being posted to a closed period without authority. The observable signal is a close audit missing a reopen authorization, reason, or accountable owner.
