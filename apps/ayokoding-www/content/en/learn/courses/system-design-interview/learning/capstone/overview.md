Complete a 40-minute, self-administered system-design interview on a fresh fictional prompt. This
capstone grades the interview performance: the full spine, a legible diagram, one targeted deep
dive, bottlenecks, trade-offs, failure behavior, and a self-score. It deliberately does not reteach
architecture mechanisms; consult [System Design](../../../system-design/overview.md) when you need
to refresh a mechanism before attempting the timed round.

## Deliverables

1. [Prompt](./prompt.md): timer, scope, and rubric.
2. [Walkthrough](./walkthrough.md): a model constructed transcript with a Mermaid diagram.
3. [Scoresheet](./scoresheet.md): ratings and evidence for each scored axis.

## Done bar

A completed attempt has a stated functional and non-functional scope, explicit estimate assumptions,
a high-level flow, an invited deep dive, a named bottleneck, two real trade-offs, a failure response,
an observability signal, and every rubric axis rated with evidence.
