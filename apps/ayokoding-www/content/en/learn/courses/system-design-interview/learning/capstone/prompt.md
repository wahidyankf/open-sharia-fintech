# Timed mock: appointment reminders

You have **40 minutes** to design a fictional service that sends an appointment reminder before a
scheduled appointment. The service must accept an appointment, let a user change or cancel it, and
avoid sending the same reminder twice. The interviewer will ask about a late delivery and a sudden
peak before a popular clinic opens bookings.

Treat all scale figures as assumptions you must ask for or propose. Do not start with a component
list: announce your plan, clarify requirements, estimate, sketch a high-level flow, choose one deep
dive, identify a bottleneck, and finish with trade-offs and next steps.

## Rubric

Rate yourself from 1 (missing) to 4 (clear and defensible) for each axis:

- scoping and requirements
- estimation
- breadth of high-level design
- targeted depth
- communication and round control
- trade-off reasoning
- reliability and failure behavior
- observability and operability
- senior/staff judgment: cost, rollout, and ownership
