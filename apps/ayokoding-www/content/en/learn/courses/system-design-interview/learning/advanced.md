The final scenarios practice a complete round and the senior/staff behaviors that make assumptions,
risk, ownership, rollout, and cost visible.

## ex-39 · design-a-url-shortener

**Concepts**: co-01–co-16.

**Scenario**: Run the full round spine on a fictional URL shortener.

**Observable check**: Include every spine stage, one deep dive, one bottleneck, and two trade-offs.

**Interview move**: Summarize the decision and its principal risk before the next question.
