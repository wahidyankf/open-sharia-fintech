## Prerequisites

- **Prior topics**: [Backend Essentials](../backend-essentials/learning/overview.md),
  [Networking Essentials](../networking-essentials/learning/overview.md), and
  [SQL Essentials](../sql-essentials/learning/overview.md) provide the building blocks this
  interview-format course assumes.
- **Tools and environment**: a timer, a plain drawing surface, and a Mermaid-capable Markdown
  renderer. There is no code runtime in this course.
- **Assumed knowledge**: professional engineering experience and a working vocabulary for HTTP,
  caches, queues, databases, replicas, and simple architecture diagrams.

## What this course teaches

This is a refresh course for experienced engineers: it assumes you already know the building blocks
and practices a legible, time-bounded system-design interview. Drive the round through a repeatable
spine: clarify requirements, estimate, sketch the high-level design, deep-dive where invited, name
bottlenecks, and state trade-offs.

**Scope boundary**: this course teaches the interview rubric and whiteboard flow, not architecture
depth. For scalable-system mechanisms, their implementation, and their production trade-offs, use
the depth course [System Design](../system-design/overview.md). Here, every scenario asks how to
make existing design knowledge visible and defensible under interview conditions.

## How the course is organized

- **Learning** supplies 22 concepts and 44 constructed, vendor-independent scored scenarios.
- **Drilling** makes the round spine automatic with recall, calculation, judgment, design, and
  why/why-not practice.
- **Capstone** is a timed mock round with a diagram and a self-score against every rubric axis.

All numbers in scenarios are illustrative assumptions, not claims about a particular company or
service. Every artifact is original instructional material rather than a transcript of a real
interview.
