## Why this exists

Posting rules make account determination reproducible from an approved event, its business facts, a dated policy, and explicit precedence. They must explain an unmatched event instead of guessing a financial account.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

Generated entries can balance while a stale policy chooses the account. The observable signal is a posting explanation whose effective policy date conflicts with the source event date.
