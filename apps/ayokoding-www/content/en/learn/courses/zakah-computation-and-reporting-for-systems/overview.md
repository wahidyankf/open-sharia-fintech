## Why this exists

AAOIFI FAS 9 treats Zakah as its own obligation, calculated from a defined asset base and reported with its own evidence. Zakah is not income tax and is not computed from the same base; a system must keep the two calculations and their provenance separate.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

A tax liability and a Zakah liability can both reconcile to cash while the Zakah base excludes a qualifying asset. The observable signal is a base-reconciliation report that cannot explain each included or excluded balance.
