## By Example

1. **Supplier invoice**: record the effective date, source link, account, amount, and narration before posting.
2. **Review queue**: separate preparer from approver and hold an entry with missing receipt evidence.
3. **Backdated correction**: reverse and re-enter an item in the documented period rather than quietly changing history.

Finish by reviewing a paper journal packet. This is an evidence-and-reasoning exercise, not posting software.
