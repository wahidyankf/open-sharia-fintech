## Why this exists

Posting must preserve evidence, account direction, effective date, and reviewer accountability. A backdated or misclassified entry can still balance while reporting the wrong period.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

A backdated or misclassified entry can preserve equal debits and credits while misstating the reporting period or account. The observable signal is a source date, approval trail, or account rationale that conflicts with the posting.
