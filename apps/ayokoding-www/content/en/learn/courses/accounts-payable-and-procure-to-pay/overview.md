## Why this exists

Procure-to-pay relies on evidence matching among a request, order, receipt, and invoice. This course teaches review and decision reasoning, not workflow implementation.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

A duplicate supplier payment can leave the ledger balanced while consuming cash twice. The observable signal is a repeated supplier, invoice reference, amount, or payment-bank trace.
