## By Example

1. **Three-way match**: compare approved order, receipt, and supplier invoice before recording the payable.
2. **Payment run**: prioritize due, approved items while retaining evidence and reviewer separation.
3. **Duplicate invoice**: identify matching supplier, reference, and amount patterns before cash leaves.

Conclude with an approve, hold, or investigate memo for a mock payment packet; do not build procure-to-pay software.
