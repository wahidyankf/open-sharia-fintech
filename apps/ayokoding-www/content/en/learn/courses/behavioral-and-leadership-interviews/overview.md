This refresh assumes prior professional experience and treats your actual work history as the raw
material. It is not a script to memorize: it is a way to make a short, honest, first-person account
of what happened, what you did, what changed, and what you learned. Senior, staff, and engineering-
manager leadership rounds are core material here, because their evidence is influence, judgment, and
collaboration—not a job title alone.

The course also makes an employment gap, a layoff, or a re-entry story ordinary interview material.
Give a truthful, bounded account when asked, name what you did next, and connect present readiness to
the role. This is communication guidance, not legal or HR advice.

Start with [Learning](/en/learn/courses/behavioral-and-leadership-interviews/learning/overview).
