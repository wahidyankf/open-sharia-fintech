## Goal

Produce a real, reusable story bank and a scored self-administered behavioral round. This is a
no-code capstone: its outputs are a written set of stories, a timed transcript, and a score sheet.
It introduces no new concepts; it integrates co-01 through co-22 under realistic interview
conditions.

## Steps

1. Complete the [story bank](./story-bank.md) with at least eight true first-person stories, including
   failure, conflict, influence, ambiguity, cross-team leadership, impact, motivation, and—when it is
   part of your history—a layoff, employment gap, or re-entry narrative.
2. Complete the [mock round](./mock-round.md): eight timed answers, including a career-context probe
   when relevant to you.
3. Complete the [score sheet](./scoresheet.md) for every answer and select one lowest-scoring axis for
   another rehearsal.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every story contains a specific action, an observable result, and a reflection.
- The mock round contains senior/staff/EM leadership evidence: influence, conflict, ambiguity, and
  cross-team alignment.
- Any layoff, employment gap, or re-entry story is factual, bounded, non-defensive, and forward-looking.
- Every answer receives a structure, ownership, specificity, result, and reflection score.

## Done bar

The completed artifact set lets a peer ask a follow-up on any story and lets you identify the one
interview dimension to improve next.
