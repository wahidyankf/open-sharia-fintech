Set a timer for 25 minutes. Answer each prompt in two to three minutes, leave time for the follow-up,
and record bullet notes rather than memorizing a script.

1. Tell me about a result you delivered under a constraint. Follow-up: what did _you_ decide?
2. Tell me about a failure. Follow-up: what changed in your practice afterward?
3. Tell me about a disagreement. Follow-up: what did the other person value?
4. Tell me about influencing without authority. Follow-up: why did people trust the proposal?
5. Tell me about ambiguity. Follow-up: what made the decision reversible or safe?
6. Tell me about cross-team leadership. Follow-up: how did you keep alignment durable?
7. Why this role, and what would you ask the interviewer to assess mutual fit?
8. If applicable, explain a layoff, employment gap, or re-entry transition. Follow-up: what demonstrates
   current readiness?

**Verify**: answers 1–6 show STAR plus reflection; answer 7 is role-specific; answer 8, when used,
is brief, truthful, bounded, and forward-looking. Do not invent a career narrative merely to practice
one—practice a truthful boundary instead.
