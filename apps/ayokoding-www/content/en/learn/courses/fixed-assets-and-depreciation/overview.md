## Why this exists

An asset balance needs evidence of existence, purpose, useful life, and impairment indicators—not only a smooth depreciation schedule.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

The ledger may remain balanced after equipment becomes idle or unusable. The observable signal is an operating, maintenance, location, or disposal record that conflicts with the register or useful-life assumption.
