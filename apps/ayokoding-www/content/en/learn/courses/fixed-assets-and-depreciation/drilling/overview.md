## Recall Q&A

Which facts make an asset record reviewable? Identity, cost, location, owner, service date, useful-life basis, and evidence.

## Scenario judgment

Can a depreciation schedule alone prove an asset still provides benefit? No; inspect impairment indicators and operating facts.
