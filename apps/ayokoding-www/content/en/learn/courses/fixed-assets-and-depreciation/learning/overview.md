## By Example

1. **Asset-register row**: record cost, location, owner, and service date.
2. **Useful-life review**: compare evidence for a revised service period.
3. **Idle equipment**: explain why an even depreciation schedule can still demand reassessment.

Prepare an asset-review memo from original facts; do not build a fixed-asset register.
