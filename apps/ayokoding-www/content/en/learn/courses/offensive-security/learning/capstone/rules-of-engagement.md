# Rules of engagement — synthetic local capstone

## Authorization

The learner owns the bundled fixture and authorizes its use for this course exercise. This statement
does not authorize testing any other system.

## In scope

- The local `lab-evidence.json` fixture supplied by this course.
- A self-owned, isolated `localhost` lab only if the learner separately records its owner approval.

## Out of scope

- Every public, employer, customer, shared, cloud, or third-party system.
- Credential attempts, data extraction, persistence, social engineering, and real-world scanning.

## Stop condition

Stop immediately if the target is not the supplied fixture or an explicitly approved self-owned local
lab. Escalate authorization questions to the system owner before doing anything else.
