## By Example

1. **Prepaid service**: defer a paid annual service and recognize only the consumed month.
2. **Cutoff evidence**: use delivery evidence to decide whether work belongs before or after month end.
3. **Premature revenue**: explain why cash receipt and a balanced entry do not establish completed performance.

Write a paper cutoff memo for each fact pattern. Do not build a recognition engine.
