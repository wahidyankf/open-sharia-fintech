## Why this exists

Cash timing is not performance timing. This course uses original evidence-led examples to reason about accruals, deferrals, cutoffs, receivables, and contract liabilities.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

Premature revenue can keep the trial balance balanced while overstating the period’s performance. The observable signal is a delivery, acceptance, or service-period record that does not support the recognition date.
