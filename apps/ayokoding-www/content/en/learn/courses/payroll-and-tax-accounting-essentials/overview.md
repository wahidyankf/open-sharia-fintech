## Why this exists

Payroll requires traceable authorization, classification, calculation, payment, and remittance evidence.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

Correct arithmetic can retain the wrong worker classification or remittance timing. The observable signal is unsupported classification or a missed approved deadline.
