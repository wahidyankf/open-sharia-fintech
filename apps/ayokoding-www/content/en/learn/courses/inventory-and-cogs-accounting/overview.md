## Why this exists

Margin depends on physical existence, ownership, movement, and cost evidence. A count and a ledger can agree yet still describe the wrong asset or cost flow.

## Silent-failure check

### What still balances while being wrong

A consistent count can still use an incorrect cost or ownership cutoff. The observable signal is consignment, shipment, title-transfer, or invoice evidence that conflicts with the recorded inventory owner or date.
