## By Example

1. **Receipt to stock**: trace a received item to ownership and inventory evidence.
2. **Release to COGS**: explain the inventory reduction associated with shipment.
3. **Consigned stock**: find an overstatement caused by ownership confusion.

Reconcile an original count, movement report, and cost schedule; do not build a warehouse system.
