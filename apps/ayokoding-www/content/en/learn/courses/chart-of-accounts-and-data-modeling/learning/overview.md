## By Example

1. **Starter chart**: group cash, liabilities, equity, revenue, and expense for one service entity.
2. **Deposit distinction**: add an account that stops customer deposits being presented as revenue.
3. **Governed change**: decide whether a proposed new account has a purpose, owner, and effective date.

This is accounting classification, not database construction or SQL instruction.
