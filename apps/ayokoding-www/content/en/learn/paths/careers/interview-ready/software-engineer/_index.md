Start here when you already build software and need to refresh the practical breadth that interviews test. Phase 1 prioritizes algorithms, design, communication, and the interview loop; the later sections deepen production systems without making that depth a prerequisite for getting ready.

Use this route as an ordered guide, not as a replacement for your professional judgment. If your foundation is current, move quickly through familiar material and spend your time practising the areas that will make your next conversation clearer and more concrete.

The linked course pages retain this path context, so the next and previous controls follow the interview-ready sequence you selected.
