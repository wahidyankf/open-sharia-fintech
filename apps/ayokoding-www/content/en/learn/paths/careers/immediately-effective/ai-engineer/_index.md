Start here when you want to build reliable AI-enabled products from first principles. The route establishes software foundations, then moves through AI application construction, evaluation, agent systems, and production model delivery.

The sequence is deliberately practical: learn to observe and test a system before relying on it, and treat deployment, evaluation, and adaptation as parts of one engineering discipline.
